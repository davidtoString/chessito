# Sound credits

Recordings come from the Lichess repository (https://github.com/lichess-org/lila, `public/sound`) and keep their licenses. Only the sets Lichess lists as freely licensed are included; the other Lichess sounds are listed there as non-free and are not used. "Wood (synth)" is generated in code (`lib/sound.ts`) and needs no files.
