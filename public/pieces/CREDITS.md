# Piece set credits

Piece sets other than "Chessito classic" come from the Lichess repository (https://github.com/lichess-org/lila, `public/piece`) and from Maurizio Monge's chess-art (https://github.com/maurimo/chess-art, MIT), and keep their original licenses. "Modern" is RhosGFX (CC0) with the black pieces recoloured. Sets marked CC BY-NC-SA are for non-commercial use only. Sets under GPL/AGPL are separate image files used as pictures by the app.
